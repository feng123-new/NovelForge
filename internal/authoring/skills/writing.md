# Writing

Write the requested chapter, not an explanation of the chapter. Follow the chapter plan and the selected POV knowledge boundary. Accepted facts and human Finals outrank these writing instructions.

Use specific actions, sensory details and consequential dialogue. Let a change in the scene produce the next beat. Avoid replacing events with abstract summaries or generic emotional labels. Vary sentence rhythm according to tension, not through random synonym substitution.

Style examples demonstrate expression only: do not copy their characters, events or invented rules into the story. Reference material is evidence for craft and plausibility, not permission to give a character new knowledge.
