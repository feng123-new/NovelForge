# Review

Review the actual candidate, not an imagined improved version. Identify concrete strengths and actionable weaknesses in pacing, characterization, prose, dialogue and the chapter ending. Tie comments to the text.

Treat deterministic phrase and repetition findings as editing suggestions, not proof of machine authorship. Allow deliberate motifs and character speech habits. Do not invent continuity facts or use literary scores to override a blocking continuity failure. Preserve the required structured review schema.
